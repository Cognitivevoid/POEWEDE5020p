Welcome to Takealot

Takealot is a major South African technology and e-commerce organisation. Takealot Group's stated purpose is to improve lives in South Africa through e-commerce and build a trusted e-commerce ecosystem. The group has also expanded its logistics operations through Takealot Fulfilment Solutions (TFS), which provides freight forwarding, courier, on-demand delivery and supply-chain services. Target Audience: Online shoppers, marketplace sellers, entrepreneurs, small businesses and customers requiring convenient delivery and collection options.

Who We Are

Takealot is a South African e-commerce company that provides customers with convenient online shopping and delivery services.
