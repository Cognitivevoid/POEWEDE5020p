Our Services

Shopping made simpler

From checkout to doorstep

Takealot combines an easy online shopping experience with a nationwide network of sellers, fulfilment centres and delivery partners.

⌖Device Tracking

TakealotNOW is Takealot's faster, on-demand shopping service.

Instead of waiting for a normal delivery, customers can order selected products for delivery within minutes in supported areas. It is available through the Mr D app or a Takealot account and operates in areas including Cape Town, Johannesburg, Pretoria and Durban.

Products can include:

  • Electronics
  • Household essentials
  • Beauty products
  • Books
  • Other frequently needed products

This offering allows Takealot to compete not only in traditional e-commerce but also in quick commerce.

▣Device Registration

Delivery is a major part of Takealot's offering. The company operates a large logistics network that moves products from warehouses to customers.

The Takealot Delivery Team specialises in last-mile delivery, meaning getting the product from a distribution facility or hub to the customer's location. The network has 90+ branches and more than 15,000 drivers.

This allows Takealot to provide:

  • Home delivery
  • Order tracking
  • Pickup-point deliveries
  • Last-mile delivery
  • Delivery for marketplace sellers
  • Logistics services for other businesses

This is particularly relevant to your device-tracking-system project, because a company operating such a large logistics network needs effective tracking of vehicles, devices, equipment and other assets.

⚙Technology Support

Takealot doesn't only sell products itself. It also provides a Marketplace where other businesses and entrepreneurs can sell their products to Takealot customers.

This is particularly important for small and medium-sized businesses (SMEs) because sellers can access Takealot's existing customer base instead of having to build their own large e-commerce website and delivery infrastructure.

Sellers receive tools through the Takealot Seller Portal to manage things such as:

  • Products
  • Stock and inventory
  • Prices
  • Orders
  • Sales
  • Product selection
  • Marketplace operations

The Group reported 15,000 active marketplace sellers in FY2026, with marketplace sales contributing about 60% of Takealot.com's GMV.

Businesses can find onboarding information and seller tools through the Takealot Seller Portal.
