About Us

Our History

Takealot’s history began in 2010, when Tiger Global Management and Kim Reid acquired an existing e-commerce business called Take2. Takealot.com was officially launched in June 2011. The company grew rapidly. In 2014, Tiger Global invested $100 million, while Takealot acquired Mr Delivery and Superbalist. In 2015, Takealot and Kalahari merged, strengthening Takealot’s position in South African online retail. Over the years, Takealot expanded its delivery network, marketplace and technology services. Today, the Takealot Group operates major businesses including Takealot.com, Mr D and Takealot Fulfilment Solutions.

Our Mission

Takealot.com’s mission is to become the most customer-centric online shopping destination in Africa. Its approach is centred around putting customers first and continually improving the shopping experience, from ordering online to receiving the product.

Our Vision

Takealot's original vision was to become the largest, simplest and most customer-centric online shopping destination in Africa. The broader Takealot Group now describes its purpose as changing lives in South Africa for the better through e-commerce, while building a trusted e-commerce ecosystem that supports customers, businesses and entrepreneurs.

Our Team

Takealot relies on a large team of technology specialists, engineers, designers, buyers, marketers, warehouse employees, drivers and other professionals. The wider Takealot Group's leadership includes executives responsible for areas such as Takealot.com, finance, technology, strategy, customer experience and supply-chain operations. Takealot also works with many external partners and businesses. Its ecosystem includes thousands of small businesses and marketplace sellers, delivery partners and other organisations. Some notable collaborations include: Pick n Pay – Takealot partnered with Pick n Pay to expand into on-demand grocery delivery. Naspers Labs – partnership focused on providing digital resources and helping address technology skills gaps among young people. GirlCode – partnership supporting women and girls in technology. Youth Employment Services (YES) – partnership supporting youth employment through internships and learnerships. Marketplace sellers and SMMEs – Takealot provides a platform that allows thousands of businesses to reach online customers.
